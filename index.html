Our story

From our family
to your family.

Poo Vaasal began as a small flower stall near the Thanjavur Big Temple, tying jasmine strings before sunrise for neighbours on their way to prayer. Three generations later, we still pick, string, and pack every order by hand — for daily needs, pooja, weddings, functions, festivals and gifts.

“எங்கள் வீட்டு மலர்கள்… உங்கள் வீட்டு மகிழ்ச்சிக்கு.”

Rooted in Thanjavur

Traditional soul,
modern hands.

Every string of jasmine we sell echoes a Thanjavur kolam — laid with the same patience, the same rhythm, the same care for the everyday. We borrow the geometry of the temple town: its bronze, its gold leaf, its early-morning stillness.
